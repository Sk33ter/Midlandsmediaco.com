About the founder

Calvin Harris

Founder & Marketing Analysis Consultant
Midlands Media Company | Lexington, South Carolina

Calvin Harris is the founder of Midlands Media Company, a Lexington, South Carolina–based marketing analysis and consulting firm focused on helping businesses understand exactly how they are being represented online—and, more importantly, where opportunities exist to improve.

Calvin approaches marketing from an analytical perspective. Rather than simply recommending advertising or social media campaigns, he begins by examining the complete picture: a company’s current online footprint, digital visibility, brand presentation, customer-facing information, and overall marketing presence.

Every Midlands Media Company client receives a comprehensive marketing audit and detailed analysis report designed to identify strengths, weaknesses, inconsistencies, missed opportunities, and areas requiring corrective action. From that analysis, Calvin develops a prioritized set of recommendations specifically tailored to the client’s business and objectives.

Those recommendations are then organized into a personalized 90-day implementation plan, giving each client a practical roadmap for moving from analysis to action. Instead of leaving clients with a list of problems and no clear direction, Midlands Media Company provides a structured strategy for addressing those issues in an organized and measurable way.

Calvin’s philosophy is straightforward: you cannot effectively improve what you have not thoroughly analyzed. By taking the time to understand where a business currently stands, he helps clients make informed marketing decisions based on their actual digital footprint rather than assumptions.

Through Midlands Media Company, Calvin’s mission is to give businesses the insight, strategy, and direction they need to correct weaknesses, capitalize on opportunities, strengthen their brand, and build a more effective presence in an increasingly competitive digital marketplace.
